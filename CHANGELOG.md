# Changelog

## 1.0 (2026-10-09)

First release.

- Save the gear you have on as a loadout (2 to 6 per character) and swap between them in one press.
- On the inventory page: a Loadouts button beside Effects & Stats opens the loadouts over the item panel, made from the
  game's own panels, fonts and item frames, with each loadout's gear, enchantments and what a swap would change.
- Enchantments move with the gear: gear coming off is disenchanted (points refunded), gear going on is enchanted and
  upgraded back to its saved tier. Only what's different is redone.
- Equip, save (asks twice to replace), rename and clear, with a controller or keyboard and mouse.
- An optional "next loadout" key to swap anywhere.
